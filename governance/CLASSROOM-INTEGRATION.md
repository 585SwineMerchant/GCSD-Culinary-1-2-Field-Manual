# Google Classroom Integration — Culinary Arts 1 & 2

## Approved operating model

The public website is the stable, version-controlled student field manual.
Google Classroom is the district-controlled assignment and submission system.
Google Drive preserves reusable assignment source files and selected portfolio
evidence. Infinite Campus remains the official gradebook.

The manual does not collect student writing, grades, submissions, or
student-identifiable information.

## Daily instructional workflow

Each instructional day may have one reusable **Daily Assignment** in Google
Classroom containing:

1. the bellringer prompt;
2. a permanent link to the exact field-manual lesson;
3. brief directions for the day;
4. the exit-ticket prompt; and
5. an individual student response document.

The manual continues to display the bellringer before the lesson and the exit
ticket after it. Students write and submit both responses in Google Classroom.

When a lesson produces a larger product, students receive a separate **Named
Assignment**. Examples include a lab plan, menu, home lab, reflection,
performance task, or assessment. Its Classroom post contains the approved
directions, student template, due date, submission requirements, manual link,
and rubric when applicable.

## Record ownership

| Record or resource | Authoritative location |
| --- | --- |
| Lessons, recipes, procedures, prompts, and unit checklists | Field manual |
| Assigned work, student documents, due dates, submissions, and feedback | Google Classroom |
| Practical performance | Teacher observation and approved rubric |
| Selected long-term evidence | Student Google Drive portfolio |
| Official grades and missing-work record | Infinite Campus |

Browser-saved unit checklist progress is a student self-management aid only. It
is not evidence of submission or completion.

## Reusable annual system

The district-owned Google Drive source library preserves approved post text,
student templates, rubrics, and teacher materials by unit and instructional day.
A student-free Master Google Classroom preserves the reusable posts themselves.

At the start of each school year, teachers:

1. receive or create live class sections;
2. reuse the prepared posts from the Master Classroom;
3. adjust dates and section-specific details; and
4. schedule or publish the posts.

Because Classroom posts link to permanent manual URLs, the public manual does
not require annual editing. Classroom API automation may be considered later,
but it is not required for launch or normal maintenance.
