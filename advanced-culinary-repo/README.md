## Guardrails

- Learning before labor.
- Preparation before independence.
- Every student completes meaningful cooking or production work.
- Team success does not replace individual evidence.
- The Cottage remains Arcadia’s local SBE identity.
- Arcadia and Olympia share technical, planning, assessment, and evidence expectations while retaining locally appropriate service models.

Exact clients, dates, menus, sales procedures, quantities, WBL-hour procedures, and local operating practices remain under department review.
